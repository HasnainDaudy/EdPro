# Product Context: EdPro

## Core Philosophy & Mechanism
EdPro is an open-source personalized education platform for K-12 learning communities. 

The central pedagogical thesis: **Children learn differently.** Forcing abstract algebraic drills onto students whose cognitive strength and passions lie in visual arts, narrative design, or acoustics causes premature disengagement and academic anxiety. EdPro establishes a functional competency baseline while allowing students to curate their curriculum around passion tracks:
- **Visual Arts & Geometry Track**: Spatial geometry, ratios, proportions, color space physics, and tessellations acquired through master studio painting, perspective drawing, and Islamic tile symmetry.
- **Game Design & Cartesian Physics Track**: Cartesian coordinates, velocity vectors, gravity acceleration, and collision algorithms acquired through video game level engineering.
- **Music & Sound Engineering Track**: Fractions, modular arithmetic, harmonics, and wave physics mastered through beat production and polyrhythms.

## Target Audience & Operational Scenes
1. **Students (Ages 6–17)**:
   - Operating in school classrooms, media centers, and home study desks under varied ambient lighting.
   - Craves agency, visual clarity, tangible feedback, and encouragement without patronizing gamification or dark-mode crypto aesthetics.
2. **Teachers & Mentors**:
   - Manages differentiated student cohorts, tracks competency progress, grades creative performance tasks using multi-dimensional rubrics, and identifies intervention needs via diagnostic heatmaps.
3. **School Administrators**:
   - Oversees COPPA consent state machines, verifies parental/school authorizations, and reviews immutable FERPA access audit ledgers.

## Structural Invariants
- **The "6+1" Standard Unit Architecture**: 6 multi-modal micro-lectures (15–20 min each) followed by 1 interactive Milestone Assessment (conceptual quiz + authentic creative artifact submission).
- **Universal Design for Learning (UDL)**: 5 contextual modalities per lesson: Interactive Reader, Video Masterclass, Hands-On Simulation Sandbox, Audio Companion, and Printable Practice Worksheet.
- **Context-Aware Socratic AI Tutor ("EdBot")**:
  - Bound to active lesson key concepts and vocabulary.
  - Age-adaptive language: Pip (Ages 6–9), Nova (Ages 10–13), Atlas (Ages 14–17).
  - Socratic invariant: Never blurts direct test/homework answers; guides with hints and analogies.
  - Deterministic PII redaction and prompt-injection interception.
- **COPPA & FERPA Compliance by Design**: Zero marketing trackers, no required email for minors <13, pseudonymous student accounts, role-based isolation.
